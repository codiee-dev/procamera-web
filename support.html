Support

Most questions are one of the ones below. If yours isn't, email us — a person reads every message.

The picture is black

ProCamera needs the camera. Open Settings → ProCamera and turn on Camera. If another app is using the camera at the same time, close it and come back.

My shots aren't in Photos

  • Check Save to Photos. In ProCamera, open Settings: Save to Photos adds every shot to your library. When it's off, shots stay in the roll only.
  • Check Photos access. Settings → ProCamera → Photos should say Add Photos Only (or Full Access). ProCamera only adds; it never reads your library.
  • The roll keeps its own copy of every shot. Open it from the last-shot thumbnail next to the shutter, and share or save from there.

Film stocks

Swipe the film shelf to try a stock: the live picture changes as you go, in photos and in video. SOFT 400, MONO 400, INSTANT SQ and HALF FRAME are free, as is shooting with no film. The other stocks preview live too; taking a shot with one asks for ProCamera Pro.

Manual controls

  • Tap a reading — ISO, SHUTTER, WB, FOCUS or EV — to open its ruler, and drag to set it.
  • Tap A on the ruler to hand that value back to auto.
  • Focus peaking outlines what's sharp in orange while the focus ruler is out. The histogram shows the light in the frame. Turn either on or off in Settings.

48 MP

Settings → Format → Resolution → 48MP. It uses the Main camera in Photo mode on iPhones whose Main camera has 48 MP; other modes and cameras shoot 12 MP. The files are about four times larger.

Subscriptions, cancelling and refunds

Purchases are billed by Apple, not by us, so cancellations and refunds go through Apple.

  • What Pro adds: every film stock, for photos and video. Everything else in the app is free.
  • Free trial: where offered, once per Apple Account. The paywall shows what you'll pay and when before you start.
  • Cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, then select ProCamera and cancel. Pro stays on until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Refund: request it at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds on Apple's behalf.
  • Restore purchases: in ProCamera, Settings → Restore purchases, or Restore on the paywall, after reinstalling or moving to a new iPhone. It restores what belongs to the Apple Account you're signed in with.

New phone and backups

The roll moves with your iPhone's own backup and Quick Start, like any app's data. Shots saved to Photos move with your photo library.
