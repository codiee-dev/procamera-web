What the app does

ProCamera is a camera. It takes photos and videos with your iPhone's cameras, lets you set ISO, shutter speed, white balance, focus and exposure by hand, and renders the picture through film stocks — looks that imitate kinds of film — live while you frame and in the files it saves.

What the camera can do depends on your iPhone. 48 MP, for example, needs a Main camera that supports it, and some controls are limited by what a particular camera allows.
