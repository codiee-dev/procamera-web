Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy describes how the ProCamera iPhone app handles information. The app is published by Mikita Kharoshka ("we", "us").

The short version

  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your photos and videos stay on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us, and we never sell or share them.
  • The camera and microphone are used only while you use the camera. Photos access is add-only: the app can save to your library but cannot see it.
  • The app does not ask for your location, and does not put it in your shots.
  • Purchases go through Apple. A subscription service, Apphud, receives purchase and technical data — never your photos or your identity.
  • There are no ads, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your device

The following is kept on your iPhone and is never transmitted to us:

  • The roll — a copy of every photo and video you take with ProCamera, with the film stock, mode and camera settings it was taken with (for example ISO, shutter speed and lens).
  • Your settings — the photo format, grid, level, histogram and focus peaking, whether to save to Photos, and the mode, film, flash and timer the camera remembers.

When Save to Photos is on, which it is by default, each shot is also added to your photo library. From there it is handled by Apple's Photos app and, if you use it, iCloud Photos, under Apple's privacy policy.

Your iPhone's own backup (iCloud or a computer) includes the roll the way it includes any app's data. Deleting a shot from the roll, or deleting the app, removes the app's copies; copies already saved to Photos stay there. When you share a shot, it goes wherever you send it with the share sheet — nothing is sent to us.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy
Camera To show the live picture and take photos and videos. The picture is processed on your iPhone to apply the film stock, the histogram and focus peaking. It is never sent anywhere.
Microphone Asked the first time you film, to record sound with your videos. Sound is recorded only while a video is recording.
Photos (add only) To save your shots to your photo library. With add-only access the app cannot see, read or change anything already in your library.

The app does not ask for, and does not use, your location, contacts or the rest of your photo library.

Purchases

ProCamera Pro is sold through the App Store and processed by Apple. We never see or handle your payment details.

We use Apphud (Apphud Inc.) to check whether a subscription is active, to deliver purchases and to show the right offer. Apphud receives technical and purchase data: device model, operating system and app version, language and country, an identifier Apphud generates for your installation, your purchase and subscription history, and which offer screens were shown. It does not receive your name, email address, or any photo, video or setting from the app. See the Apphud privacy policy.

Advertising and tracking

The app shows no ads, does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your requests

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of data relating to you, and to object to certain processing. Everything the app keeps is on your iPhone and is yours to change or delete. For the purchase data held by Apphud, write to us and we will have it deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version is published on this page with a new date at the top.
