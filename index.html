Film camera for iPhone

See the film before you shoot.

Expo puts the film on the live picture, so what you see is what you get. Set ISO, shutter, white balance and focus by hand, or leave them on auto. No account, and your shots stay on your iPhone.

Get it on the App Store

What's inside

  • Fifteen film stocks — colour negative, slide, black and white, instant, cine and tape — live on the picture, in photos and in video
  • ISO, shutter, white balance, focus and exposure on one ruler, each with a way back to auto
  • A live histogram, focus peaking, a grid and a level
  • 48 MP from the Main camera, in HEIF or JPEG
  • Photo, video, portrait, night and slow motion
  • A roll that remembers each shot's film, ISO and shutter

The camera, every tool and four film stocks are free. Expo Pro unlocks the rest of the film shelf.
